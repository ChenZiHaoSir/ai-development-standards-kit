# 01 Discover

Interview in small batches. Ask only questions whose answers change the product, constraints, risk, or implementation.

Resolve:

- who has the problem and what they do today;
- the smallest valuable outcome;
- primary journeys and failure cases;
- in-scope and explicitly out-of-scope behavior;
- data, privacy, security, performance, budget, and platform constraints;
- success metrics and acceptance evidence;
- decisions needed now versus later.

Write `docs/specs/00-brief.md`. Do not start implementation until the user confirms it.

Exit gate: summarize assumptions, unresolved decisions, risks, and the proposed first vertical slice.
